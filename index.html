PUBG MOBILE

PUBG MOBILE is a globally popular Battle Royale mobile game co-developed by LIGHTSPEED STUDIOS of Tencent Games and KRAFTON, Inc., and officially licensed from PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS, originally created by Brendan Greene.

First released globally in March 2018, PUBG MOBILE brought the intense PlayerUnknown’s Battlegrounds experience to mobile devices with detailed environments, tactical combat, vehicles, weapons, and multiple maps powered by Unreal Engine 4. In the classic Battle Royale, up to 100 players drop onto the battlefield, gather weapons and supplies, outplay opponents, and survive the shrinking play zone. **Only one player or team can claim victory — become the last one standing and earn the iconic #1 Winner Winner Chicken Dinner. From Erangel and Miramar to Sanhok and Vikendi, PUBG MOBILE continues to connect players through competitive gameplay, events, collaborations, Royale Passes, and unforgettable battles.

At its heart, PUBG MOBILE is about strategy, survival, teamwork, and the pursuit of one final victory.
